What IV Hub is

IV Hub is a volunteer rostering site for Ismaili Volunteers at Auckland Jamatkhana. It builds duty rosters, shows volunteers their duties, and records attendance. It is run by volunteers for volunteers and there is no charge to use it.

This demo

The demo is a preview of a proposed design. Its people and events are made up, and nothing you do in it is saved or sent anywhere. It is provided for review only.

Accounts

  • Accounts are created by invitation from a Director or Facilitator. There is no public sign-up.
  • Your account is for you alone. Do not share your password.
  • Your role decides what you can see and change. Roles are set by the people above you in the chain of command.
  • An account can be removed, or its access changed, by a leader with the authority to do so.

Using it properly

  • Use the names, contact details and attendance records you can see only for organising volunteer duties.
  • Do not copy other people's details out of the Hub for any other purpose.
  • Record attendance and roster changes accurately. Changes are logged with your name.
  • Do not try to reach pages or records your role does not allow.

Rosters and duties

Rosters are produced by the Hub from rules set by the IV leadership and can be changed by Team Leads. A roster is a plan. If you cannot make a duty, tell your Team Lead and record your away dates.

Availability

IV Hub depends on outside services for its database, sign-in and hosting. It may be unavailable at times and features may change.
