Privacy Policy

IV Hub, Ismaili Volunteers, Auckland Jamatkhana. Draft for review, prepared 13 November 2026.

Draft. This page describes how IV Hub works today, written from the software itself. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer or approved by the leadership of Ismaili Volunteers Auckland. Items marked "To confirm" need a decision before this is published.

Who this covers

IV Hub is the rostering site used by Ismaili Volunteers at Auckland Jamatkhana. It is used by volunteers and by the leaders who organise them. It is open by invitation only.

This demo

The demo you may be viewing uses made-up people and events. It does not connect to the IV Hub database. Anything you change is kept in your own browser tab and is gone when the tab closes. The demo sets no cookies and loads no analytics.

What IV Hub holds

InformationWhat it is used for
Your account: email address, display name, role, and the time you last opened the HubSigning you in, showing you the pages your role allows, and telling leaders whether a roster has been seen
Your member record: name, team, gender, age group, date of birth, phone, email, rank, specialised teams, and any notes a leader has addedBuilding rosters that follow the duty rules, and letting your leaders contact you
Away dates and duty preferencesKeeping you off the roster on days you cannot make, and honouring preferences where it can

Audit log: the action taken, who took it (display name and email) and whenBeing able to see who changed a roster, a member or an account
View log: who opened a section that holds other people's details, and whenAccountability for access to personal details
Announcements written by leadersSharing notices with a team

IV Hub does not hold payment details. Passwords are handled by the sign-in provider and cannot be read by leaders or by the people who maintain the site.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own duties, attendance, contact details and away dates.
  • Published rosters show names against duties to people signed in to the Hub.
  • Team Leads and Assistant Leads can see the member records and attendance of their team.
  • Facilitators, Directors and the site Owner can see records across teams, manage accounts, and read the audit log.

Where it is kept

  • The database and sign-in are provided by Supabase.
  • The site's pages are served from GitHub Pages.
  • The live site loads one code library from the jsDelivr network and its typeface from Google Fonts. Those services receive your IP address when the page loads, as any website you visit does.
  • Your browser keeps a copy of your sign-in session and your light or dark setting.

Children and young people

IV Hub holds records for volunteers under 18, including date of birth and age group, because the duty rules depend on age.

To confirm. How a parent or guardian agrees to a young volunteer's details being held, and who may view them.

How long it is kept

To confirm. IV Hub has no automatic deletion today. A decision is needed on how long member records, attendance and logs are kept after someone stops volunteering.

Seeing and correcting your information

You can view and edit your own contact details on the My Profile page. For anything else, ask your Team Lead. Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you may ask to see the personal information held about you and ask for it to be corrected.
